# 2026-09-26: Koottam starts

**Idea (Roshan):** put several AI models on different servers, make them interact, and get
a new, better model out of it.

**Shape chosen:** a council (several models vote, an aggregator breaks ties), then
distillation (fine-tune a small model on the council's filtered answers). Open-weight
teachers only, so training on their outputs raises no licence problems. Budget under $10.

**Scope call:** Roshan asked for cyber security, AI-takeover defence, war/disaster
prediction and "all of the above". v1 covers **defensive security** plus
**prompt-injection detection** (the buildable part of "defence against AI"). Offensive
tooling is out. Forecasting wars or disasters is out: language models can't forecast, so it
went to the vault inbox as a separate idea.

## Done

- Repo scaffolded: config-driven council over OpenAI-compatible APIs, per-server rate
  limiter with 429/Retry-After backoff, and a resumable answer cache.
- Data: CyberMetric (Apache-2.0) and deepset/prompt-injections (Apache-2.0). SecQA and
  CTIBench were rejected for training because they are CC-BY-NC-SA.
- Split: test = CyberMetric-500 + deepset test (616); train = 10,150.
- **Caught a leak:** one test question sat in the training pool as a typo'd copy.
  Exact-match dedupe missed it, so a near-duplicate (Jaccard ≥ 0.8) filter was added,
  which removed 60 more training questions.
- **Measured:** local Gemma 4 E2B on laptop CPU takes ~60 s/question with thinking on and
  ~13 s with `reasoning_effort=none` (420 vs 64 output tokens). The config gained a
  per-model `extra` field; request settings are part of the cache key.
- Free catalogues checked live: OpenRouter has Gemma 4 31B, Nemotron 3 Super and Qwen 3.8
  27B as `:free`; Cerebras has gpt-oss-120b (5 RPM, 1M tokens/day).
- 18 offline tests pass; ruff and mypy (strict) are clean.
